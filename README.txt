FREE HOSTING WITH GITHUB PAGES
1. Go to https://github.com/ and sign in/create a free account.
2. Create a NEW public repository, for example: bubuu-anniversary
3. Upload index.html and the entire images folder.
4. Open the repository's Settings → Pages.
5. Under "Build and deployment", choose "Deploy from a branch".
6. Select the main branch and the /(root) folder, then Save.
7. Wait a few minutes. GitHub will show your website URL under Pages.
8. Open that URL on your phone to test it, then send it to Bubuu.

IMPORTANT:
Keep the images folder next to index.html. Do not rename the image files unless you also change their names in index.html.
